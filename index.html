Custom software

The exact scripts used in this study are collected, byte-identical and with checksums, in the code/ folder of this repository. The stand-alone tools are also maintained as separate repositories; the versions used carry the tag tal-manuscript-2026:

  • sear2k — sear, SSEARCH36-based genome search
  • SINE_orth_loc — orthologous presence/absence of loci

Subfamily names, the copy numbers of Figure 2 and the tribe numbers of Table 2 are explained in the README.
